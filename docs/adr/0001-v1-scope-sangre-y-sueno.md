# 0001. v1 scope: only the Sangre y Sueño universe

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Requirements:** RF-01, RF-03

## Context

The archive spans several books, records, drawings and videos, but there is one developer and a very small budget. "Sangre y Sueño" also names several works: the 1981 poetry book (*Relatos poéticos de Diego Calahumana*), the 1996 songbook, the 1984 LP and the 1993–95 home recordings.

## Decision

v1 publishes only *Sangre y Sueño*, treating the poetry book, the songbook and the recordings as **one work with several parts**. Other works are shown as "coming soon".

## Consequences

A complete, polished experience is a better funding pitch than a large but half-finished archive.

## Rejected alternatives

Publishing the whole archive at once; treating the poetry book and the LP as separate works.
