# 0005. The prototype doubles as a funding demo

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Requirements:** RF-03, RF-11, RF-62

## Context

The project will seek further funding once a working prototype exists.

## Decision

Design for impact: a strong home page, the full *Sangre y Sueño* experience, future works visible as "coming soon", and a public analytics dashboard.

## Consequences

Shows the potential of the whole project and provides evidence of use.

## Rejected alternatives

A minimal prototype without its own design.
