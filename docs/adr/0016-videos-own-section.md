# 0016. Own video section with every available video

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Requirements:** RF-02, RF-30, RF-31, RT-24

## Context

The videos (*Los Caminos del Hombre*, documentaries) are not part of *Sangre y Sueño*.

## Decision

Every rights-cleared video is published in its own section, behind a facade: thumbnail first, `youtube-nocookie` player loaded on click.

## Consequences

Shows the breadth of his work; YouTube hosting is free; the facade keeps pages light and private.

## Rejected alternatives

Only the 1996 presentation; embedding the YouTube iframe directly.
