# 0034. Songs without a recording show a note

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Requirements:** RF-18

## Context

Some recordings are missing (e.g. 3 tracks from the 1984 LP).

## Decision

The song is shown with its lyrics and drawings and the note "Grabación no disponible en el archivo".

## Consequences

Completes the songbook and makes missing material visible.

## Rejected alternatives

Hiding songs without audio.
