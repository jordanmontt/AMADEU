# 0010. Editorial, archival visual identity

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Requirements:** RF-45, RF-46

## Context

The site needs a tone that represents Marcelo and does not look like a generic template.

## Decision

Warm paper tones, serif type for texts, his own drawings as graphic identity, La Paz and the Illimani on the home page, and an animated hero.

## Consequences

Gives the archive its own character and sets it apart for funders.

## Rejected alternatives

A generic app or museum template.
